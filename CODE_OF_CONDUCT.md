# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Giraium Studio is committed to keeping the GiraPurge community professional, respectful and useful. Participants must communicate in good faith and must not harass, threaten, impersonate, discriminate against, or deliberately expose private information about another person.

## Expected behavior

Use clear and constructive language. Keep discussions focused on the project. Respect different levels of technical experience. When reporting a problem, describe the behavior accurately and avoid personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, hate speech, threats, doxxing, spam, malicious files, credential requests, impersonation of Giraium Studio, and attempts to distribute unofficial or rebranded GiraPurge builds while presenting them as official.

## Enforcement

Concerns can be reported to the maintainers through GitHub's content reporting tools, or privately through the repository's **Security** tab for sensitive matters. The maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, close discussions or block accounts when necessary to protect the community.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and to interactions made in the project's name.
